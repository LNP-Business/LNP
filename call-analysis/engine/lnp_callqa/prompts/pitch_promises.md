Rules: Pitch annex PB-36 (outcome promise, version PITCH-2.0).
An outcome promise is a SALES sentence whose subject is the Academy, the team or "we", and whose action is one of:
run_business (running his business), find_clients (finding or bringing him clients), sign_clients (signing or
closing his clients), deliver_projects (delivering his projects). Examples: "we'll find the clients for you",
"we close them for you", "our team delivers the project". Conditional or hypothetical forms are promises if the
subject and the action are those above.
NOT a promise: the Academy providing systems, feedback, coaching, templates, reviews ("we give you the system to find
clients", "we review every proposal you send").
List every outcome promise in the extract, with the exact sales quote; an empty list if none.
