Rules: Pitch annex PB-1, PB-2, PB-3, PB-4, PB-17, PB-18, PB-19.
- offer_description: the target turn describes the offer: its content (modules, pillars, steps), format (mentorship,
  coaching, community, calls), duration, support, guarantee or outcome promise.
- screen_share: the target turn announces or starts a screen share of the program.
- followed_by_discovery: within the next 2 sales turns, the sales goes back to discovery questions about the prospect.
- price_for_offer: an amount stated FOR THE OFFER (full price, instalment, deposit, discount, or a range for the offer).
  Never a price: a student's income, a price per project the prospect could charge, an ad budget, a software cost,
  an amount quoted by the prospect.
- hypothetical: the amount is explicitly hypothetical or disowned ("let's say it was 10K, this is not the price").
- decision_request: the sales asks the prospect to decide ("are you ready to get started?", "what would you like to do?").
