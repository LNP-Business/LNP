# Objections · nominate resistances without a marker (OB-1)

A code inventory already flagged the prospect turns that contain a typical resistance marker. Your job: list the
other PROSPECT or OTHER turns of the extract that express a resistance although they contain no listed marker.

A resistance is a passage where the prospect (or a co-decider labelled OTHER) expresses:
- an objection: a doubt about the offer, its price or terms, the company, the sales, or a resource they lack
  (time, money, a co-decider's agreement);
- an anxiety: a doubt about themselves (not managing, not lasting) or about their decision (choosing wrong, being fooled);
- an inertia: attachment to the present, or a postponement without an external reason.

Never nominate (OB-2): a request for information without a reserve in the same turn; a delay with an external,
verifiable reason (a move, a contract end); a neutral decision-circuit fact ("my husband handles the money");
a conditional acceptance ("if I can pay monthly, then yes").
Never nominate a turn listed under ALREADY FLAGGED.
