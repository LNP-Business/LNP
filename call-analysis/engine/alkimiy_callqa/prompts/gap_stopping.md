Rules: Gap annex GB-30 step 1 (stopping question).
A stopping question asks the prospect what stops them, what has held them back, what gets in the way, or why they
have not done it yet ("What's held you back?", "What's stopping you?", "What's getting in the way?", "Why haven't
you done it yet?", "What internally has prevented you from taking steps?").
- `is_stopping_question`: true or false.
- `quote`: the exact words of the question in the sales turn.
