"kind", one of:
- "holds": the turn repeats the resistance, explains it, gives details on it or adds weight to it: the doubt is still
  there ("c'est quand même beaucoup", "je n'ai pas cet argent pour l'instant", answering « par rapport à quoi ? » with
  why it is too much). An answer to the sales' question about the resistance that describes it is "holds".
- "no_longer_holds": the prospect says in their own words that the resistance no longer holds, or restates it as
  answered ("ok, si je peux payer en quatre fois, c'est faisable pour moi", "du coup ce n'est plus un problème").
- "commits": the prospect commits to an answer the sales proposed (a payment plan, a date, a call with the spouse),
  with a specific element (an amount, a number of instalments, a date, a first step): put that element's words in
  "specific". Without a specific element of their own, it is "agrees".
- "moves_forward": the prospect moves forward on their own initiative, beyond the resistance (asks how to start, asks
  for the payment link, proposes a date).
- "agrees": the prospect accepts the sales' answer to THIS resistance without content of their own ("ok", "d'accord",
  "je vois", "oui", "ça marche").
- "asks": a question about the sales' answer or the offer, with no acceptance and no refusal (checks an amount, asks a
  detail).
- "other": the turn is about something else (another subject the sales moved to, another resistance, small talk). An
  « ok » or « oui » that answers the sales' NEW subject is "other", not "agrees".

"prompted" (for "no_longer_holds" and "agrees"): true when the content of the resolution was first stated by the sales
and the prospect only approves it ("donc le paiement en plusieurs fois, ça te va ?" → "oui").
"quote" (required for holds, no_longer_holds, commits, moves_forward): the prospect's exact words in that turn, 3 to 25
words, copied character for character. No quote for agrees, asks, other.
