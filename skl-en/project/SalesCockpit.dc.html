Sales cockpit

1What this page is for

The sales view of SKL OS in one screen: how leads turn into cash, what each call and each sale costs and brings, how every sales rep is doing, what is coming in the next 30 days and which cash is at risk. It is the same view as Cockpit › Sales.

2How it helps

  • From lead to cash shows where the funnel leaks, step by step, so the team works on the weakest step first.
  • Unit economics follows the last 8 weeks against the baseline (dashed line): what a call, a sale and a client cost and return.
  • Sales team ranks the sales reps by cash; a click opens their pipeline in the CRM.
  • What's coming and Cash at risk say what to prepare and what to secure this month.

3How to use it

  1. Read it top to bottom on Monday morning and at the Saturday review.
  2. Hover a figure to see how it is computed; click a sales rep to open their leads in the CRM.
  3. Open the CRM (in the sidebar) to act on a lead: declare an outcome, follow up, add a note.
