Weekly Review

1What this page is for

The Weekly Review is the Saturday ritual that keeps the quarter on track: 45 minutes, the whole team, always the same 6 steps, in this order: the tasks (alert triage), the milestones, the key results, then the scoreboard, the decisions and next week's milestones. Everything is read from the shared data (key results, alerts, decisions, tasks) and everything decided in the room is written back to it. Each closed review is kept in History with a snapshot of the key results, the milestones of the week and those made official for the next one.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • Numbers before opinions. Every KR is checked in before it is discussed.
  • No red KR without a plan. A red or untracked KR leaves the room with a plan, an owner and a date.
  • Nothing stuck survives Saturday. Every Critical and High alert still open gets an outcome in front of the team.
  • Milestones closed on facts. Each weekly milestone is judged on its mini goals: all ticked is done, anything else is not done with its cause (estimation, dependency, priority, skill, commitment).
  • Milestones you can count on. Each week's milestones are made official on Saturday and closed on their mini goals the next Saturday: the share reached is the team's reliability.

3How to use it

  1. Check-ins are done on Saturday: each KR owner saves the check-in in step 3 before the review. The red count in the sidebar shows the KRs still missing.
  2. On Saturday, the facilitator runs the 6 steps in order on a shared screen. The left rail shows what is done.
  3. Step 1 settles the alerts and the tasks still open this week (Delete asks once: press Enter). Step 2 ticks the mini goals of each weekly milestone, then marks it done or not done. Step 3 records each key result's new value next to its actual value. Step 6 makes next week's milestones official; a key result may have none (the validation first shows its risk, the milestones it reached and its last 3 weeks). Step 6 is required to close; the others can stay open: the gaps are recorded with the review.
  4. Close the review. It moves to History, and next Saturday its milestones come back in step 2.
