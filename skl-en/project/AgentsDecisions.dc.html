Agent System · Decisions

1What this page is for

The human control of the agents: approve, edit or reject what they propose, and handle the escalations.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • Agents propose, humans decide. Every output an agent is not yet trusted to send alone waits here for a person, and every decision teaches the agent.
  • Autonomy is earned. An agent moves up a level only when its figured criterion is met: tests passed and outputs approved without edits.
  • One owner per agent. Every agent has a person who answers for it and a person who receives what it must not handle.
  • Everything is traced. Every run keeps its trigger, result, duration and cost.

3How to use it

  1. Pick an item in the list on the left.
  2. Read why it needs you, the agent's proposal, its destination and the sources it read.
  3. Approve to send it, or reject it: the next pending item opens on its own.
  4. Decided items stay in the list, faded, with the decision recorded.
