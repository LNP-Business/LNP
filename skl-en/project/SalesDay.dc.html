Sales Day

1What this page is for

The sales rep's day in the CRM, one lead at a time: every lead that needs you today comes as a card, in the order that protects the cash. First the outcomes to declare (a call is over and nothing is declared), then today's calls to prepare, the reschedules to plan (an R2 not held, a canceled call), the no-shows to re-engage and the follow-ups due.

2How it helps

  • Nothing falls through. The queue is built from the CRM itself, so a call never stays without its outcome and a lead never waits without a follow-up.
  • One thing at a time. Like Execute, you see one lead, the action to take and what you need to take it, nothing else.
  • The CRM stays true. An outcome declared here is the same as in CRM › Sales cycle; "Done" saves what you did as a note on the lead.

3How to use it

  1. Start the day here. Choose My leads (or a sales rep, for the leads).
  2. For an outcome: say if the call was held, then the decision (and the reason for a no), then Declare and next.
  3. For the other cards: do the action, write in one line what you did, then Done or swipe the card to the right.
  4. Tomorrow puts a lead aside for today, Skip sends it to the end of the queue; Up next lets you pick another lead first.
  5. Your list of the day (done, put aside) is kept for you only.
