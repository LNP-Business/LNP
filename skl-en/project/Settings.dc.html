Alerts & notifications

1What this page is for

This is where the OS alert system is set. Each alert watches one condition, such as a task going overdue, a key result turning red or a decision past its deadline. For each alert you choose the threshold, the severity, who gets notified and through which channel. Delivery rules decide when notifications arrive and what happens when nobody reacts.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • The system pushes, nobody has to check. A slip reaches the right person as it happens, not at the next meeting.
  • The right person, not everyone. Each alert goes to the owner first. Romain only gets what needs him, or what nobody handled in time.
  • No noise. Severity, quiet hours and the daily digest keep low-value alerts from interrupting deep work.
  • Rules that match how you work. Thresholds follow the OS rules (8 h tasks, 3 tasks in progress, 48 h decisions) and can be tuned as the team matures.

3How to use it

  1. Switch an alert on or off with the toggle on its line.
  2. Click the alert's name to open its settings.
  3. In the popup, adjust the threshold with − and +, for example how many hours after the committed date a task counts as overdue.
  4. Pick the severity (Low alerts go into the daily digest), who is notified and the channels. The dark box shows exactly what will be sent.
  5. Set the delivery rules: quiet hours, daily digest and the escalation delay.
  6. Save. Review the settings at each quarterly planning: an alert that nobody acts on should be tuned or switched off.
