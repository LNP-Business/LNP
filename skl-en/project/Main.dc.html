Cockpit

1What this page is for

The Cockpit is SKL's control tower. In under 60 seconds it answers three questions: Will we hit this quarter's objectives? What's slowing execution? What's waiting on me? The switcher at the top opens a department view: Marketing, Sales or Finance.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • Manage by exception. What's on track stays quiet; only gaps take up space. Attention goes where it changes the outcome.
  • Project, don't just observe. Every KR shows its forecast landing and required pace. We correct in week 4, not week 13.
  • One signal, one action. Every alert has its button: decide, follow up, reassign. Nothing is left hanging.
  • Visible bottlenecks. Blockers, team load and pending decisions, including the founder's, are all measured.
  • Zero prep. Saturday's Cockpit is the weekly review agenda.

3How to use it

  1. Each morning, scan the KPI row: cycle time, promise reliability, blockers, load, decision speed.
  2. Handle “Waiting on you” first: these are blockers only you can clear.
  3. Move to “What's slowing execution” and trigger the suggested action.
  4. Review the KRs: a red KR requires a recovery plan within 48 h; a KR with no check-in for 7 days gets a follow-up.
  5. Check explorations: a used-up timebox forces a decision (continue, pivot or stop).
  6. On Saturdays, walk through the Cockpit as the weekly review agenda.
