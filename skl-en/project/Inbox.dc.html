Page method

Inbox

1What this page is for

The Inbox is the single place where every alert of the OS lands: key results turning red or not checked in, tasks blocked or late, decisions overdue, people overloaded. The rules come from Settings; the data comes from Roadmap, My Day and Decisions. Nobody has to hunt for problems across screens.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • One owner per alert. Each alert goes to the person it concerns. It can be taken or reassigned, and the name is visible to the whole team.
  • Nothing stays stuck in silence. A Critical or High alert with no action beyond the escalation delay goes up to the lead automatically.
  • No noise. Snooze pushes an alert to a date; it comes back on its own. Medium and Low alerts never escalate.
  • Closed by the facts. You mark an alert handled, but it only leaves for good when the data is fixed: the task unblocked, the KR checked in, the decision taken.

3How to use it

  1. Start each day on the “Mine” tab. The red count in the sidebar is what waits on you.
  2. Click an alert to see its detail, its escalation clock and its history.
  3. Open it where it lives and fix it, or take it, assign it, snooze it, turn it into a decision or create a fix task.
  4. Leads check the “Escalated” tab every morning. On Friday, the Weekly Review triages every Critical and High alert still open.
