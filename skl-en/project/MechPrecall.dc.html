Pre-call mechanism

1What this page is for

This page models the Pre-call mechanism with the Iteration system: 21 organs spread across 5 functions (frame, matter, machinery, control, epistemic). From that model the page computes the stocks and flows, checks 5 gates, and lists the frictions that should become decisions.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • You act on causes, not symptoms. The diagnosis points to the organ that limits the result, so effort goes to the one lever that matters.
  • No iteration on a broken model. The gates stop you from testing a lever while the model leaks units or corrects faster than the system can answer.
  • Facts are separated from hunches. Every value carries its evidence level, so nobody defends a guess with the confidence of a measurement.
  • One shared picture. Anyone can read how the mechanism works, who owns which lever and what is deliberately left out.

3How to use it

  1. Build: follow the steps to construct the mechanism, one organ at a time.
  2. Model: read the graph left to right. Click a node to edit it; the bar on top holds what frames the mechanism.
  3. Organs: the 21 organs by function, each with its explanation and filling rules.
  4. Control: the gates, the ignorance map and the notifications. Fix what blocks first, then open a decision.
  5. After each test, update the organs it changed: the model improves with every cycle.
