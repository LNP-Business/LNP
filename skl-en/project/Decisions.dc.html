Decisions

1What this page is for

This is the backlog of every SKL decision: pending, made and up for review. Each decision has its record: the question, the cause to address, the options, the assumption, the expected outcome, a single decision owner and a deadline. Each record is linked to the key results it serves, the tasks it blocks or triggers, and the deliverables it produces or changes.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • No more issues that drag on. 48 h for a reversible decision, 7 days for an irreversible one. Past that, an alert is raised.
  • One decision owner. Every decision has a named owner: no more re-debating the same topics every week.
  • Full traceability. Links show what a decision unblocks, which result it moves and which documents it changed. Nobody works on a task that waits for a decision without knowing it.
  • Decisions that get better. The 30- and 90-day reviews compare the actual outcome to the expected one and capture the lesson.

3How to use it

  1. Read the board left to right: To decide, Decided, To review. Click a card to open its full record on the right.
  2. Every decision follows 5 steps: the problem, 3 weighted variables (weights add up to 100), the context of each variable, 3 options scored on each variable, then the roadmap (tasks, key result, deliverables).
  3. Decisions taken more than 2 weeks ago move to the Archive, next to “New decision”.
  4. Any question that blocks at least one task becomes a decision.
  5. Frame it as a choice, with at least two real options, and state whether it is reversible.
  6. Link it: the key result it serves, the tasks it blocks, the deliverables it will produce or change.
  7. The decision owner decides before the deadline and writes down the expected outcome.
  8. Every exploration that reaches the end of its timebox is closed out here: continue, pivot or stop.
  9. In “To review”, compare actual to expected, note the lesson and update the linked playbook.
