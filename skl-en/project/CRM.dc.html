CRM

1What this page is for

The sales CRM, taken as is from ALKIMIY Lite: every prospect from first contact to cash collected, with its next action, its calls, its transcripts and the reason it was lost.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • No prospect falls through. Every lead carries a next action with a date; late ones stand out.
  • The pipeline is honest. Stages, rounds and loss reasons are explicit, so the forecast rests on facts.
  • Cash, not just deals. Closed deals show invoiced and collected amounts side by side.

3How to use it

  1. Pipeline: drag a card to move a prospect to its new stage.
  2. Leads: filter, sort, pick your columns and act on several leads at once.
  3. Sales cycle: one line per live lead, from the booked call to the first Whop payment, against the Show, Decision and Cash targets. Each morning, read Critical, then Sales must act, top to bottom; Calendar shows today and the next calls, Since booking compares cycle lengths; open a line to read every interaction, or the lead record for every message, call and payment.
  4. Click a prospect to open its file: calls, next action, note, transcripts and analysis.
  5. Log the next action before you close a prospect's file.
