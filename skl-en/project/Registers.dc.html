Registers

1What this page is for

Registers hold what several mechanisms share: the interfaces where one mechanism hands units to the next, the resources they compete for, the events that break comparisons, and the people who own the levers. A mechanism sheet describes one mechanism; a register describes what sits between them.

2How it makes everyone more efficient

  • No orphan leaks. If Pre-call sends 61 shows and Sales counts 90 bookings, the Interfaces register shows the gap nobody owns.
  • Hidden contention becomes visible. Two mechanisms that slow down together often share a saturated resource with no arrow between them.
  • Honest comparisons. The Events register tells you which before/after comparison is no longer valid.
  • The real human bottleneck. The Owners register shows how many levers depend on the same two or three people.

3How to use it

  1. Pick a register with the four tabs at the top.
  2. Read the four boxes: what the register holds, what it detects, how it filters iterations and when to fill it in.
  3. Settle the highlighted lines first: they are open questions written as TO ARBITRATE, CONFIRM or FILL IN.
  4. Watch the Resources load bars: a resource past its alert threshold blocks any iteration that adds volume.
  5. Log an event the day it happens: it takes thirty seconds and saves a wrong conclusion.
  6. Click a mechanism name to open its model in the Knowledge System.
