Framework 01 · Knowledge System · IP

{{fw.t1}}

The base sits on top: mechanisms are what you are trying to make more efficient, and everything below exists to serve them. The loop reads the base, works left to right, and every result returns into it.

4 steps3 exits10 blocks

What it is

one sentence, four steps

An iteration system is a closed loop that reads a base of mechanisms and registers, turns each friction into a decision someone can make, tests one lever at a time, and writes every result back — amending, correcting or extending the very base it reads.

An open loop is not an iteration system. Running tests without writing the results back produces activity, not learning: every cycle restarts from the same level of ignorance and the same arguments come round again. The write-back is what makes the loop reinforcing — richer base, better-founded hypotheses, fewer inconclusive tests, capacity freed, more iterations closed.

Every block of the loop

role, how it works, what it is not, rules
the base

Mechanisms

Its role

What runs and produces a result: chains and systems. This is what everything else in the arrangement exists to make more efficient.

How it works

Each mechanism is modelled with 21 organs, serving five functions. The loop reads them at diagnose and writes to them at write back — those are the only two points of contact.

What it is not

Not a foundation you pour once. If the write-back never touches it, the loop is open and every cycle restarts from the same level of ignorance.

Rules to apply
  1. A mechanism is ready for the loop once all five functions are served and the two laws are checked. Below that, diagnosing produces noise.
  2. One primary result per mechanism. Two competing results mean two mechanisms, or a trade-off nobody made.
  3. No two mechanisms produce the same thing. If they do, the boundary between them is badly drawn.
  4. A mechanism is only worth formalising if its delay exceeds your patience. What answers in two hours is steered by eye.
the base

Registers

Its role

What belongs to no mechanism in particular. Four shared lists: interfaces, resources, events, owners.

How it works

A register holds an object exactly once. Mechanisms populate them; the loop reads them at diagnose to check the two blockers that no single mechanism can see.

What it is not

Not a preamble. Create them before modelling so shared objects land in them, but their content comes from the mechanisms — populate resources first and you list what you believe is scarce rather than what your mechanisms contend for.

Rules to apply
  1. What belongs to nobody is watched by nobody. That is the whole reason a register exists.
  2. Events and owners start immediately: a date cannot be reconstructed, and authority is a fact you already know.
  3. Resources and interfaces fill themselves as mechanisms arrive. An interface cannot be written before two mechanisms touch.
  4. Position decides where an object lives, not its dynamics. A shared stock — cash, context base, audience — belongs in resources.
layer

Measure

Its role

Make quantities observable, continuously. It sits under the base rather than inside the loop.

How it works

Sensors read on their own cadence, independent of whether an iteration is running. Every value carries its evidence level and its date: measured, benchmark, hunch.

What it is not

Not a step of the loop. Making it one implies you measure once per cycle, which is exactly what the noise rule warns against — you need a series, not a reading.

Rules to apply
  1. Instrument the accumulations, not only the output. A swelling stock precedes the effect instead of recording it.
  2. Measure noise over a period without intervention, otherwise you measure your own corrections.
  3. Three readings minimum before trusting a trend.
  4. A value past its shelf life changes level: a six-month-old measurement is a historical benchmark.
step 01

Diagnose

Its role

Turn the base into a finite list of named problems. The only step that runs entirely on structure — no judgement, only comparison.

How it works

Two laws are checked first: cadence against total delay, dead band against sensor noise. Then the ignorance map ranks what you do not know by what it carries. Then eleven friction rules read the organs — conservation, clock mismatch, saturation, blind gap, runway, and the rest.

What it is not

Not a review meeting. Nothing here needs an opinion. If a rule requires judgement to fire, it is not a rule, it is a discussion.

Rules to apply
  1. Laws before heuristics. A broken law makes the reading of every other friction unreliable.
  2. A friction that points at no organ is not a friction.
  3. Rank by ignorance weighted by what it carries, never by expected gain.
  4. Diagnose across the whole base. Two of the four blockers are invisible from inside a single mechanism.
step 02

Decide

Its role

Convert a friction into a call someone is allowed to make. This is where diagnosis becomes action.

How it works

Each friction carries a blocked question, two or three options with their cost and their owner, and the caller taken from organ 05. The reversibility lane is set here: reversible under a week goes light, committing goes through the full gates.

What it is not

Not a prioritisation exercise. Ranking comes after elimination — a decision blocked by a saturated resource or a slow stock is removed before anything is scored.

Rules to apply
  1. No decision without a named caller. A decision everyone owns is one nobody makes.
  2. Two options minimum, and one of them is always « accept and document ».
  3. Eliminate before ranking. The four blockers are checked before any scoring.
  4. The lane is chosen before the test, never after seeing the result.
step 03

Iterate

Its role

Move one lever and read what happens, under conditions where the reading can mean something.

How it works

One lever, one window, one measurement comparable to the one before. The test runs at least as long as the turnover of the stock it crosses. The decision criterion is written before it starts and applied unchanged afterwards.

What it is not

Not an experiment for its own sake. A test that cannot come back negative is not a test, it is a rollout with extra steps.

Rules to apply
  1. One lever per cycle. Two make attribution impossible and the cycle is wasted.
  2. Duration is set by the stock, never by patience.
  3. The criterion is written before the start and applied unchanged after.
  4. Log every context change during the run. That log separates a wrong mechanism from a polluted test.
step 04

Write back

Its role

The only step that closes the loop. It is what makes the whole thing compound rather than repeat.

How it works

Three exits: amend a value, fix the structure, extend the base. Whatever the verdict, something is written — a failed test still moves the prior.

What it is not

Not documentation. Documentation records what happened; the write-back changes what the next diagnosis will read.

Rules to apply
  1. Write back whatever the verdict.
  2. Fixing structure invalidates every earlier diagnosis on that mechanism. Re-run them.
  3. Extend only when the clock test forces it. If the lever is merely outside the boundary, redraw the boundary instead.
  4. Any event during the run goes to the events register, with its date.
exit 1

Amend values

Its role

The ordinary case, and the most frequent. A measured value replaces a hunch, a delay is finally known, a prior moves.

How it works

The value and its date go into organ 19 of the mechanism concerned. Its evidence level changes: hunch becomes measured, or measured becomes a historical benchmark once past its shelf life.

What it is not

Not a correction of the model. The structure stands; only a number moved.

Rules to apply
  1. Nothing you concluded before becomes wrong. The next diagnosis simply reads better numbers.
  2. A value error is self-healing: measure again and it moves toward the truth.
  3. Always carry the date. A value without one cannot be aged out.
  4. Record the posterior even when the verdict is inconclusive.
exit 2

Fix structure

Its role

The test revealed the model itself was wrong: a missing stock, a boundary drawn too narrow, an assumption of independence that fell.

How it works

Correct the organ concerned — 01 for the boundary, 08 for a stock, 20 for an assumption — then re-run the diagnosis on that mechanism from scratch.

What it is not

Not an amendment. A shape error is a different animal from a value error and must not be treated as one.

Rules to apply
  1. Every earlier diagnosis on that mechanism becomes suspect. Frictions you dismissed may now fire.
  2. A shape error never self-corrects: each iteration refines the parameters of a wrong form and confirms it with growing confidence.
  3. This is why the ignorance map ranks shape above value, despite being far rarer.
  4. One structural fix outweighs fifty amendments.
exit 3

Extend base

Its role

A mechanism that did not exist has to be created. The library grows from evidence rather than intention.

How it works

Almost always because the clock test forced a split: two halves running at speeds differing by more than a factor of five, so one cadence cannot pilot both.

What it is not

Not the answer to a lever sitting outside the boundary. That calls for redrawing the boundary, not for a new mechanism.

Rules to apply
  1. Extend only when the clock test forces it. Otherwise redraw.
  2. Record why: split, or redrawn boundary. Nothing else explains why a mechanism exists.
  3. New interfaces appear and resources gain one more claimant. Update both registers.
  4. Multiplying mechanisms because a lever was out of reach is how a library stops being maintainable.

One turn, in practice

what a Monday morning looks like
  1. 1
    Open the control screen

    Two laws first: cadence against total delay, dead band against sensor noise. If either is broken, stop — every other reading is unreliable until it is fixed.

    2 minutes
  2. 2
    Read the ignorance map, not the friction list

    It is ranked by what each unknown carries: an unserved function, then shape, then bound, then value. A shape assumption outranks a wrong number even though it is rarer.

    5 minutes
  3. 3
    Take the top item to the decision backlog

    Write the blocked question, two or three options with their cost, and the caller. Then set the lane: reversible under a week, or committing.

    10 minutes
  4. 4
    Check the four blockers before launching

    Slow stock, blind sensor, saturated resource, busy owner. Any one of them and the test cannot produce a usable answer — drop it and take the next item.

    2 minutes
  5. 5
    Run one lever, for at least the turnover of the stock it crosses

    Write the verdict criterion before you start. Log any context change during the run — that log is what will separate a wrong mechanism from a polluted test.

    days to weeks
  6. 6
    Close it, whatever the verdict

    Record the prior and the posterior. Then take one of the three exits: amend the value, fix the structure, or extend the base. An iteration closed without a write-back leaves nothing.

    10 minutes
Everything before the run takes under half an hour. The expensive part is the test itself, and its duration is set by the stock it crosses rather than by your patience. If a turn is taking longer than that on the desk work, the model is being rebuilt rather than read — which means it was not ready to enter the loop.

Where the gap comes from

the loop has two entry points
First pass

You name it: an observed value, a target, a date. This is phase one of modelling, not a step of the loop — it happens once, when the mechanism is built.

Every pass after that

The previous write-back hands it to you. A value moved, an assumption fell, a structure was corrected — and the next diagnosis reads a different base, raising frictions the last one could not.

Why it matters

« Starting from the mechanism models what is available. Starting from the gap models what matters. »

What the loop measures about itself

built into the decision backlog
The flow layer

Work in progress, cycle time, throughput, inconclusive rate. Little's law: cycle time equals work in progress divided by throughput, so every extra decision held in test lengthens all the others proportionally. The WIP limit is the only lever that shortens cycle time without changing anything else.

The reversibility lane

A change you can undo in a week and one that commits three months should not carry the same overhead. Reversible ships and gets logged; committing goes through the full gates with a named caller. Without the split the method is too heavy everywhere, which is how method tools die by month three.

Confidence, not verdict

A binary verdict throws information away — two inconclusive tests can carry very different evidence. Recording the prior and the posterior turns a failed test into a partial answer, and lowers the confidence of every future iteration resting on the same mechanism.

The iteration system is itself a mechanism. It has stocks — decisions open, in test, pending — a delay, and noise. It is the one thing in the company that would otherwise be exempt from its own method, which is why the backlog carries these numbers rather than a task list.
Framework 02 · Knowledge System · IP

{{fw.t2}}

A mechanism is a chain or a system. Same organs in both — what separates them is a single return path, and everything else follows from that.

2 shapes4 roles6 phases

Definition

one sentence

A mechanism is a chain or a system — a set of elements that interact to produce an intended result, regularly enough that it can be steered rather than rebuilt every time.

Interact

This is what separates a mechanism from a sequence. If the elements follow one another without ever influencing each other back, you have a chain of steps, and its behaviour is deducible from its parts.

test — is there at least one closed loop?
Intended result

Intended, not pursued. A mechanism wants nothing: it produces a result, and that result is not always the declared one. The distance between the two is an object in its own right — organ 03.

test — what does it actually produce when pressure rises?
Regularly enough

This is what makes steering possible. You only steer what repeats: on a one-off event there is nothing to tune, only a decision to make once.

test — does it start again, at an identifiable cadence?
The mechanism is not the model. The mechanism exists and runs whether you wrote it down or not: your sales mechanism is turning today with its sales reps and its CRM. The model is your representation of it, and it can be wrong. Conflating the two makes it impossible to say a model is mistaken, hence impossible to correct it.

The two shapes, same organs

same notation as the model graph
matter flowrule governing a flowmeasurementcommand
Only stocks carry matter. A rule does not sit in the path — it governs a flow from the side, which is why it is drawn with a dotted line onto the arrow rather than as a box between two stocks. Same for levers and the policy: they command, they do not transport.

The same matter path, plus four organs that close the loop. Sensors, gap, policy and levers are what the system has and the chain does not — and all four are constructs: they do not exist in the real thing, you build them. A chain does not become a system by being more complicated. It becomes one when you build the loop it was missing.

The loop is a deliverable, not an entry requirement. A chain enters your library as a candidate. The second role — absorbing variation without mobilising anyone — needs a regulation loop: a dead band, a cadence, a policy. So modelling takes something that already runs and grafts on the loop it lacks. That is why four organs are typed « construct »: sensors, gap, policy and owner do not exist in the real thing, you build them.

What is not a mechanism

five things people model by mistake
Not thisWhy, and where it belongs insteadThe question that settles it
A toolA CRM, a spreadsheet, an automation. It carries no matter of its own — it is where other mechanisms' sensors live. Operations register, or organ 15.does it produce a result, or does it hold someone else's data?
A teamPeople are an owner and a capacity, not a mechanism. Modelling « the sales team » rather than « the sales call » draws a boundary around an org chart instead of around a loop.would the boundary survive a reorganisation?
A projectIt has an end. A mechanism starts again. A launch is a project; what it leaves running afterwards may be a mechanism.does it recur at an identifiable cadence?
An eventIt does not repeat, so it cannot be steered — only logged. Anything that returns every year is not an event either: it is an unmodelled input.will it happen again in the same form?
A goal« Growth », « quality », « being the best ». A result is not a mechanism; the thing that produces it is.can you name what enters and what leaves?
The common thread is that none of them has an input and an output. That is the cheapest test available: name what enters, name what leaves, and say at what cadence it starts again. If you cannot do all three, whatever you are looking at belongs in a register, in an organ of some other mechanism, or nowhere.

The four roles

each with its test
  1. 1
    Produce its result repeatably

    The first term of the definition put to the test. A project ends, a mechanism starts again. Until the result has a name, a unit and a cadence, you have an activity, not a mechanism.

    test — can you name what it produces, in what unit, at what cadence?
    Checked by · answered by organs 10 and 02 — outputs give the unit, the declared objective gives the cadence
  2. 2
    Absorb variation through its own interactions

    The role everyone forgets, and it follows directly from the word « interact ». A well-tuned mechanism takes small variations on its internal loops without mobilising anyone. If it demands a human call on every gap, it is not a mechanism: it is a queue of decisions dressed up as a process.

    test — what happens when the gap stays small? If the answer is not « nothing, it is written down », the role is not met.
    Checked by · answered by organ 17 — the dead band, and the inaction written next to it
  3. 3
    Signal its own drift before the result shows it

    Interactions produce delayed effects: by the time output falls, the cause is often weeks old. A swelling stock precedes the effect instead of recording it, which is why a mechanism must be instrumented on its accumulations, not only on its output.

    test — what signal appears first when this mechanism starts to degrade?
    Checked by · answered by organs 08 and 15 — a stock with a sensor on it, not just a sensor on the output
  4. 4
    Be runnable by someone other than you

    A mechanism only you can run is not a mechanism, it is a personal skill. This is the role that decides whether the company can grow: delegating assumes a named owner, a written cadence and a fixed dead band.

    test — how long before someone else takes it over, and what do they need that is not in your head?
    Checked by · answered by organs 05 and 17 — a named owner who can hold a refusal, and a policy decided in cold blood

Ready for the loop

the gate between building and iterating

A mechanism enters the loop once all five functions are served and the two laws are checked. Not twenty-one organs.

The five

Frame, Matter, Machinery, Control, Epistemic. Every organ serves exactly one, and nothing in the diagnosis works while one is unserved.

Below the gate

Diagnosing produces noise. The rules have no inputs, so the frictions they raise are artefacts of what is missing rather than of what is wrong.

Above the gate

The loop turns with most organs still empty — and it fills them itself, through the write-back. Completeness is an output of iterating, not a prerequisite.

The gate is the same for a chain and for a system. Depth of modelling differs — a chain rarely needs every organ, a system usually does — but the five functions are what the rules read, and no diagnosis runs without them either way.

Modelling

6 phases, each producing its share of the model
  1. 1
    Frame the gap

    An observed value, a target, a date. Until all three are written, you do not have a problem but a concern, and nothing downstream can trigger a decision.

    produces · Organ 16 · Gap, and its target term, organ 02
  2. 2
    Draw the boundary

    Draw it around a complete loop, never around a team. Then check the clock ratio: two halves running at very different speeds are two mechanisms.

    produces · Organ 01 · Boundary, and its complement organ 21
  3. 3
    Trace the matter

    Outputs first, then stocks, then flows, then inputs. That order is deliberate: starting with inputs models what is available rather than what matters.

    produces · The model graph — organs 06, 08, 09, 10
  4. 4
    Open the machinery

    Transformation rules, the split between levers and frozen parameters, delays. Delays come last because they depend on the stocks identified in the previous phase.

    produces · Organs 04, 05, 11, 12, 13, 14
  5. 5
    Close the loop

    Sensors with their latency and noise, gap, policy. The policy is set last because its cadence is computed from the total delay established just before.

    produces · The control apparatus — organs 15, 16, 17, 18
  6. 6
    Qualify

    Evidence level for every value, structural assumptions. This phase produces no new component: it annotates what exists, and it is the only one that stops a model being relitigated forever.

    produces · The ignorance map — organs 19 and 20

Library rules

once you have several
  1. Each mechanism has one primary result. Two competing results mean either two mechanisms, or a trade-off nobody wanted to make.
  2. No two mechanisms produce the same thing. If they do, the boundary between them is badly drawn, and you will have permanent attribution fights over results.
  3. A mechanism is only worth formalising if its delay exceeds your patience. On something that answers in two hours, trial and error beats the model.
Framework 03 · Knowledge System · IP

{{fw.t3}}

A mechanism holds what is inside it. A register holds what is between mechanisms, or above all of them. Four registers, and the reason they exist is blunt.

4 registers3 positions2 blockers

What a register is

one sentence

A register is a single shared list of what belongs to no mechanism in particular — because what belongs to nobody is watched by nobody, and described inside each sheet a shared object is entered twice and the two copies diverge.

The failure this prevents, in one example. Your pre-call mechanism reports 61 leads leaving. Your sales mechanism declares 90 arriving. Twenty-nine units vanish between them, and neither sheet is wrong on its own — each is impeccable inside its own boundary. Nobody does the subtraction because there is nowhere the two numbers meet. In an interface register there is one number, and the discrepancy is impossible to miss.

Where an object lives

position decides, not dynamics
PositionWhat sits thereHeld byWhy
Inside a mechanismStocks, flows, levers, sensors. They belong to one mechanism and live in its organs.organs 06 to 18No register. Duplication is impossible — there is only one owner.
Between mechanismsInterfaces, when a flow passes from one to another. Resources, when several draw on the same pool without passing anything.interfaces · resourcesBelongs to neither. Written inside each, it is entered twice and the copies diverge.
Above all mechanismsEvents, which break comparability everywhere at once. Owners, whose authority cuts across sheets.events · ownersBelongs to no mechanism at all. Only a shared list can hold it.

The four registers

what each holds and detects

Interfaces

the point where what leaves one mechanism enters another
What it holds

Interface point, sender or senders with their share, receiver or receivers, mode — duplication or split — rate, unit of time, and the agreed definition.

What it detects

Orphan leaks. If one mechanism reports 90 leaving and the next reports 61 arriving, twenty-nine units vanish between them — and neither sheet is wrong on its own.

Filter it hands to iterations

« This iteration changes what the neighbour receives: warn them, and measure on the slower of the two clocks. »

The point to remember

It belongs to neither mechanism. Described inside each, it is entered twice and the two copies diverge. In the register it exists exactly once.

Resources

what several mechanisms compete for without passing it between them
What it holds

Name, nature among the five — throughput, threshold, depleting stock, stock threshold, presence — unit, capacity, load, alert threshold, and who draws on it.

What it detects

Contention. Two mechanisms slow down together with no visible internal cause, and nobody connects them because there is no arrow to follow.

Filter it hands to iterations

« Do not iterate to raise volume here: the resource is saturated and the gain will be absorbed by the queue. »

The point to remember

Cross-cutting stocks live here too — cash, the context base, audience, reputation. Position decides where an object lives, not its dynamics.

Events

what breaks comparability
What it holds

Date, the event, what it breaks, and the mechanisms affected. Four columns, no modelling.

What it detects

Nothing, and that is deliberate. It produces an invalidation rather than a friction: measurements before and after are no longer comparable.

Filter it hands to iterations

« Do not compare across this date. »

The point to remember

Anything that returns every year — seasonality, holidays — is not an event. It is an unmodelled input and belongs in organ 06.

Owners

who holds authority over which lever
What it holds

Person, role, the levers under their authority, the mechanisms involved, and whether they can hold a refusal.

What it detects

The real human constraint. It is usually the attention of two or three people, rather than budget or time, that bounds the whole.

Filter it hands to iterations

« This iteration has no available owner, whatever its priority. »

The point to remember

Authority cuts across mechanisms. Duplicated inside each sheet, the concentration on one person stays invisible.

What is not a register

three cases that look like one
CaseWhere it belongsNuance
A stock inside one mechanismIt lives between two flows of that mechanism. Organ 08, not a register.unless it is shared — cash, context base, audience — in which case position wins over dynamics and it moves to resources
An invalidated assumptionIt concerns one mechanism's structure. Organ 20 holds it.it is the only asset a failed test produces, but it is not transverse
A metric definition« Lead shown » means what exactly? It belongs to the sensor that produces it, organ 15.arguably the weakest call in this classification — two teams comparing numbers on different definitions never reconcile
The test is position, not dynamics. A stock and a resource behave differently — one accumulates and circulates, the other bounds without circulating — but that is not what decides where they live. What decides is whether more than one mechanism touches it. A shared stock goes to the resources register and keeps its stock nature there, which is why that register carries the five natures rather than a single one.

Filling order

create all four, fill them at different times
  1. Create all four before you model. Not to fill them, but so shared objects have somewhere to land instead of being duplicated inside each sheet. A register that does not exist when you draw your first mechanism will never catch up.
  2. Events start today. A date cannot be reconstructed afterwards, and a missing event ruins a conclusion months later — you will not know whether a mechanism was wrong or a test was polluted.
  3. Owners start today too. Authority is a fact about your organisation that you already know without modelling anything.
  4. Resources fill from the second mechanism. Populate them before and you list what you believe is scarce rather than what your mechanisms actually contend for.
  5. Interfaces fill from the third. You cannot write one before two mechanisms touch — it is the only register that is strictly derived.
Two of the four blockers live here. A saturated resource and a busy owner are the reasons not to iterate that no single mechanism can see. Which also means these two registers only start paying once three or four mechanisms are modelled — below that, the slow stock and the blind sensor carry the load alone.

Cuts what belongs to the mechanism from what stays outside. Everything else in the canvas depends on that cut.

06

Inputs

Function

Feeds the mechanism with resources, signals or units to process.

Control question

What, how much, at what cadence, with what variance?

If the organ is missing

You blame the process for a drop that comes from upstream, and tune an engine starved of fuel.

Filled in when

An average rate and a spread, not a running total.

07

Disturbances

Function

Represents the shocks the mechanism takes without choosing them: seasonality, platform, market.

Control question

Which shocks, how often, of what amplitude?

If the organ is missing

The model looks more stable than it is. Real variance surprises you every cycle.

Filled in when

Each disturbance has an estimated frequency and a quantified effect on a flow or a stock.

08

Stocks

Function

Accumulates between two flows. The mechanism's memory, and the thing that reveals bottlenecks.

Control question

What piles up, at what level, for how long?

If the organ is missing

The bottleneck goes invisible: conversion rates stay fine while the queue grows.

Filled in when

A level and an average age, recorded on a date.

09

Flows

Function

Carries matter from one step to the next, at a given rate and pass-through ratio.

Control question

What rate between each step, and what pass-through ratio?

If the organ is missing

You reason on global volume without knowing where it jams. Corrections become random.

Filled in when

Every edge on the canvas carries a rate and a ratio.

10

Outputs

Function

Produces the measurable result to be compared with the objective, and which sometimes feeds another mechanism.

Control question

What result, measured how, going to whom?

If the organ is missing

The mechanism runs with no proof it serves anything. Activity gets mistaken for production.

Filled in when

A metric, a unit, a destination.

Machinery · 4 organs
11

Levers

Function

Variables genuinely actionable over the chosen horizon, with their range and their cost.

Control question

What can you move over this horizon, within what range, at what cost?

If the organ is missing

The diagnosis leads to no action. You know what is wrong and can do nothing with it.

Filled in when

Each lever has a current value, a range and a cost of movement.

12

Frozen parameters

Function

Fixes what will not move over the horizon, and therefore enters the simulation as a constant.

Control question

What is frozen for this horizon, and for what reason?

If the organ is missing

You plan on imaginary levers. The plan is unworkable from day one.

Filled in when

Each frozen parameter carries a reason and a review date.

13

Transformation rules

Function

Links each step's input and output through an explicit function.

Control question

What function links input and output at each step, with what coefficients?

If the organ is missing

The simulation has no engine. You stack ratios without knowing why they take those values.

Filled in when

You can compute a step's output from its input without looking at history.

14

Delays

Function

Shifts the effect relative to the cause. This is what sets the maximum steering cadence.

Control question

How long between the action and its visible effect on the output?

If the organ is missing

You judge too early, stack corrections, and the mechanism starts oscillating instead of converging.

Filled in when

One delay per step, in time units, measured rather than assumed.

Control · 4 organs
15

Sensors

Function

Makes a quantity observable, with an instrument, a latency and a noise level.

Control question

What quantity, measured by what instrument, with what latency?

If the organ is missing

The mechanism is endured, not steered. You react to visible consequences, always late.

Filled in when

Every stock, output and leak has at least one named sensor.

16

Gap

Function

Compares measured against target. The only signal allowed to trigger an action.

Control question

What value, over what window, with what trend?

If the organ is missing

You react to anecdote instead of signal, and every wobble becomes grounds for intervention.

Filled in when

A signed number, together with its observation window.

17

Policy

Function

Converts a gap into action, following a rule decided in cold blood.

Control question

What rule triggers what action, from what threshold?

If the organ is missing

Every gap triggers a different improvisation. No learning is possible between cycles.

Filled in when

A default action is written for every signal on the control table.

18

Loops

Function

Closed paths in the mechanism. A B loop pulls back to target, an R loop amplifies the gap.

Control question

Which closed loops exist, and which are balancing, which reinforcing?

If the organ is missing

You treat as linear a mechanism that feeds itself. Compound effects arrive unanticipated.

Filled in when

Every loop is drawn on the canvas and labelled B or R.

Epistemic · 3 organs
19

Evidence level

Function

Qualifies how solid each value in the model is, and flags where the risk concentrates.

Control question

Is this value measured, estimated or assumed, and on what sample?

If the organ is missing

An assumption acquires the status of fact. The model looks solid while resting on hunches.

Filled in when

Every simulation variable carries an evidence tag.

20

Structural assumptions

Function

Lists the causal links accepted without testing, and the observation that would invalidate them.

Control question

Which causal links do you take for granted without having tested them?

If the organ is missing

When the model fails, you do not know what to fix: the structure or the parameters.

Filled in when

Each assumption has a decisive test written next to it.

21

Excluded scope

Function

Explicitly records what was left out of the model, and why.

Control question

What did you deliberately leave out, and under what condition should it come back?

If the organ is missing

Surprises arrive with no known origin. Every anomaly restarts an investigation from scratch.

Filled in when

The list exists and gets re-read before any counter-intuitive conclusion.

The ten proposed organs, settled

eight absorbed, two still open
ProposedWhat it wasVerdictWhere it went
Horizon and cadenceA parameter is only « frozen » relative to a duration.absorbedOrgan 17 — cadence is already one of the policy's three settings, and the horizon is the objective's.
Binary success criterionThe threshold that turns an objective into a test.absorbedOrgan 02 — the acceptable minimum field does exactly this.
Resources consumedBudget, time and attention burnt each cycle.absorbedThe resources register — it is transverse, so it never belonged inside a mechanism.
Leaks and scrapDrop-offs, churn, dead leads, content never shipped.absorbedOrgan 10 — induced outputs, and the conservation gate makes them impossible to skip.
Capacity and bottleneckThe maximum rate of each step.absorbedOrgan 04 — the throughput nature, with its degradation threshold.
Non-linearitiesThresholds, saturations, diminishing returns.absorbedOrgan 13 — the non-linear flag, and a gate that fails when none is declared.
ActuatorWhat actually executes the decision, with its own reliability and delay. A policy with no actuator changes nothing.openGenuinely missing. The owner says who may decide; nothing says what carries it out, or how long that takes.
Measurement latencyData age, noise, sensor bias.absorbedOrgan 15 — all three are now mandatory fields of a sensor.
Known failure modesThe ways this mechanism breaks, with the leading signal for each.openNothing holds it. It would make monitoring predictive rather than reactive.
Decisive testWhat observation would invalidate a structural assumption.absorbedOrgan 20 — the test field next to each assumption.
The derivation settled eight of the ten. Once the five functions were named, most of these turned out to belong to something that already existed rather than new objects. Two survive: the actuator, and the list of known failure modes. Both are real gaps, and neither has a home yet.
